# Portfolio Experience

This context defines the visitor-facing surfaces and language of the AVABBBB portfolio.

## Language

**Opening Hero**:
A standalone entry surface placed before Home. It creates the site's first visual impression and offers one clear transition into Home.
_Avoid_: Hero page, splash screen, Home

**Home**:
The existing primary content surface reached after the Opening Hero, containing the creator introduction, focus areas, featured work, and routes into the rest of the portfolio.
_Avoid_: Opening Hero, splash page

**AIGC Technical Reader**:
The Opening Hero's primary visitor: someone seeking practical, evidence-backed ideas about AIGC engineering and creative production workflows.
_Avoid_: General audience, recruiter

**Visit Session**:
A browser-tab visit during which the Opening Hero appears at most once. It is unrelated to authentication or account state.
_Avoid_: Login session, user session

**Generative Type Field**:
The Opening Hero's signature visual: prompt fragments, characters, and work signals that react to the visitor and assemble into the AVABBBB identity.
_Avoid_: Particle background, neural-network animation

**Work Signal**:
A curated, verifiable fragment of the creator's real AIGC practice, used inside the Generative Type Field as a preview of the site's substance.
_Avoid_: Decorative AI jargon, random code, invented metrics

**Signal Point**:
The red interactive anchor of the Opening Hero. It focuses the Generative Type Field and expands into the cream reveal that transitions to Home.
_Avoid_: Decorative dot, custom cursor, loading indicator

**Language Preference**:
The visitor's persisted choice between Chinese and English, shared by the Opening Hero and Home. On a first visit it is inferred from the browser language.
_Avoid_: Opening language, component-local language

**Opening Entry Rate**:
A coarse trend comparing visits to the Opening Hero at `/` with subsequent visits to Home at `/home`. It is not a session-correlated click conversion rate.
_Avoid_: Exact conversion rate, Enter click rate
